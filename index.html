What is addition with regrouping?
👦
🤖
Great question! 💡 Regrouping (also called "carrying") is when you have 10 or more in one place value column, so you carry the 10 over to the next column!
Example: 17 + 5
• 7 + 5 = 12 (Write 2, carry 1)
• 1 + 1 = 2 tens
• Total = 22 🎉
Ask Doubt>Concept Visual

Step-by-Step Visual: Addition with Regrouping

Step 1

Line up the Numbers

Tens | Ones 1 | 7+0 | 5

Always align ones with ones and tens with tens.

Step 2

Add the Ones (Regroup)

7 + 5 = 12Keep 2, Carry 1 ⬆

12 is 1 ten and 2 ones.

Step 3

Add the Tens

1 (carry) + 1 = 2Final Sum = 22

Combine tens for the complete answer!

📝 Revision Sheet: Addition & Place Value

🌟 Key Rules to Remember

  • Order Property: Changing the order of numbers does not change the sum (e.g. 4 + 3 = 3 + 4 = 7).
  • Zero Property: Adding 0 to any number gives the same number (e.g. 9 + 0 = 9).
  • Regrouping Rule: Whenever a column totals 10 or more, carry the ten to the left.

⚡ Quick Mental Math Tip

To add 9 quickly, add 10 and subtract 1! Example: 25 + 9 = 25 + 10 - 1 = 34.
